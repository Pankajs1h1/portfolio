Academic Focus

Research Areas

Focusing on modern probabilistic frameworks, stochastic dynamics, and large deviation asymptotics for mathematical models.

Primary Research Areas

Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs)

Modeling dynamic systems subject to random noise, analyzing existence, uniqueness of strong solutions, and asymptotic behavior.

$$\mathrm{d}X_t = b(t, X_t)\mathrm{d}t + \sigma(t, X_t)\mathrm{d}W_t$$

Large Deviation Principle (LDP)

Investigating asymptotic properties of rare events using weak convergence techniques established by Budhiraja and Dupuis.

$$\mathbb{P}(X_\epsilon \in A) \approx \exp\left(-\frac{1}{\epsilon} \inf_{x \in A} I(x)\right)$$

Probability Theory

Measure-theoretic probability, infinite dimensional spaces, Polish spaces, and stochastic perturbations in dynamic systems.

$$\mathbb{E}[M_t \mid \mathcal{F}_s] = M_s \quad (s \le t)$$

Related & Foundational Interests

Mathematical Finance

Risk metrics, option pricing dynamics, financial econometrics, and stochastic volatility modeling in financial markets.

$$\mathrm{d}S_t = \mu S_t \mathrm{d}t + \sigma S_t \mathrm{d}W_t$$

Stochastic Processes

Analysis of Markov chains, Brownian motion, martingale representations, and application to biological population models.

$$\mathbb{P}(X_n = x_n \mid X_{n-1} = x_{n-1}, \dots) = \mathbb{P}(X_n = x_n \mid X_{n-1} = x_{n-1})$$

Reaction-Diffusion Equations

Analysis of spatial reaction-diffusion PDEs featuring anti-predator dynamics and fear effects under stochastic environmental noise.

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = D \nabla^2 u + f(u) + \sigma(u)\dot{W}$$
